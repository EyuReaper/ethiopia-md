# About Ethiopia.md: Origin and Purpose

Ethiopia.md is an open, free knowledge base about Ethiopia that anyone can improve. It covers history, geography, culture, food, art, music, technology, nature, people, society, the economy and everyday life. Articles are written as plain Markdown files, so people can read them on the website, developers can use them in code, and AI systems can read them directly. This page explains what the project is, where it came from and how to take part.

## Why an Ethiopia knowledge base?

Ethiopia is a country of more than 120 million people, with more than 80 languages and its own Ge'ez script. Yet most of what the internet says about Ethiopia is written in foreign languages, from an outside perspective. Organized, sourced content written in Amharic is very scarce. Search engines and language models answer questions about Ethiopia from that same thin material. Ethiopia.md is an attempt to close that gap by gathering sourced articles that people and machines can both trust in one open place.

## Origins: a fork of Taiwan.md

Ethiopia.md did not start from scratch. It began as a fork of **Taiwan.md**, an open-source knowledge base about Taiwan built on the same idea. From that project it inherited the site's technical structure (Astro), the way articles are organized, the translation system and the editorial guidelines. It did not inherit the content. The Taiwan articles have been taken off the live site and are being replaced, one by one, with real, sourced Ethiopia content. That migration is not finished: for now, what is live is mainly the 12 category introductions (Hubs). Thanks are due to the Taiwan.md maintainers and contributors for sharing their work as open source.

## Amharic first

In this project, **Amharic is the single source of truth**. Every article is written first in Amharic under the `knowledge/` directory; the English, Spanish and Japanese versions are translations of the Amharic. Where two versions disagree, the Amharic one wins. Dates on the Amharic site are shown in the Ethiopian calendar. The aim is to make sure Ethiopian readers can read about their own country in their own language first.

## Editorial principles

- **Cite sources:** every significant claim should have a verifiable source.
- **Leave gaps rather than guess:** if the information isn't available, an empty section is better than a guessed one.
- **Respect different perspectives:** Ethiopia has many ethnic groups and faiths, so contested topics are presented with balance.
- **Three layers of depth:** articles move from a short overview to detailed sections, related topics and sources.

## How to contribute

Ethiopia.md is open on GitHub: [github.com/EyuReaper/ethiopia-md](https://github.com/EyuReaper/ethiopia-md). The project is maintained by the GitHub user **EyuReaper**. Anyone can take part:

1. **Report a problem:** if you find wrong information, a broken link or leftover content about another country, [open an issue](https://github.com/EyuReaper/ethiopia-md/issues).
2. **Write a new article:** create a new Amharic file under `knowledge/{Category}/` and send a pull request. Frontmatter with a title, description, date and tags is required.
3. **Translate:** translate published Amharic articles into English, Spanish or Japanese under `knowledge/en/`, `knowledge/es/` or `knowledge/ja/`.
4. **Review:** read other people's pull requests and check the facts and the quality of the language.

Beyond this, the project has no confirmed partner organization, sponsor or permanent team yet. If that changes, it will be stated plainly on this page.
