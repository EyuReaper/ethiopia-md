# The Battle of Adwa: Sovereign Defense and the Icon of Anti-Colonial Resistance

On March 1, 1896 (Yekatit 23, 1888 in the Ethiopian calendar), in the mountains around the town of Adwa (ዓድዋ) in Tigray, an Ethiopian army under Emperor Menelik II (ዳግማዊ ምኒልክ) routed an invading Italian force and brought Italy's war of conquest in the Horn of Africa to an end. [Harvard University Press: Jonas, The Battle of Adwa](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674062795) At the height of the European partition of Africa, the result kept Ethiopia independent and challenged the assumption that every African state would sooner or later fall under European rule. The historian Bahru Zewde calls it the most important military victory in modern Ethiopian history, and a turning point that mattered for the wider Black world as well as for Ethiopia. [Bahru Zewde, Africa Review of Books: Adwa, a Milestone in the Creation of Modern Ethiopia](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

## Origins: The Treaty of Wuchale and Article 17

The conflict grew out of the Treaty of Wuchale (Wechale, ውጫሌ), signed on May 2, 1889 by Menelik and the Italian envoy Count Pietro Antonelli. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) Under it, Menelik recognized Italy's claim to what became its colony of Eritrea in exchange for a loan of arms and money. [History.com: The Battle of Adwa](https://www.history.com/articles/battle-adwa-ethiopia-africa-colonization) The treaty also gave the Italians their first foothold on the edge of the northern highlands. [Library of Congress Country Studies: The Reign of Menelik II](https://countrystudies.us/ethiopia/15.htm)

The dispute centered on Article 17, which read differently in its two versions. The Amharic text said Ethiopia _could_ use the Italian government for its dealings with other powers. The Italian text was read as obliging it to do so, which Rome took to mean that Ethiopia was an Italian protectorate. [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/battle-adwa-ethiopia-africa-colonization) Historians still disagree over whether Antonelli built the discrepancy in on purpose or acted in good faith. The Swedish historian Sven Rubenson and the Italian scholar Carlo Giglio debated the question in the _Journal of African History_ in the 1960s. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

Bahru also points to a second grievance that writers on Wuchale often overlook. Article 3 set the boundary between Ethiopia and Italian Eritrea, and steady Italian encroachment beyond that line was what finally pushed Menelik to war. His mobilization edict complained that "the enemy has been eating into our territory like a mole". [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

## The Road to War, 1894–1895

In 1894 the Italians' former vassal Bahta Hagos of Akkele Guzay rebelled against them. In late 1894 and early 1895 the Italian commander, General Oreste Baratieri, defeated the Tigrayan ruler Ras Mengesha Yohannes at Coatit and Senafe and pushed deep into Ethiopian territory. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) In September 1895 Menelik issued his call to arms, the _kitet_ (ክተት). Forces were raised from across the empire, and estimates of the army that eventually marched north run from about 73,000 to more than 100,000 men. [Wikipedia: Battle of Adwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Adwa) [History of War: Battle of Adwa](https://www.historyofwar.org/articles/battles_adwa.html) Bahru argues that Menelik's real advantage was the modern rifles he had bought, paid for with the resources and trade routes of the southwestern provinces and Harar, which he had brought under his control in the 1880s. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

## Amba Alage and the Siege of Mekelle

The campaign had three main engagements. At Amba Alage on December 7, 1895, the Italian vanguard was almost wiped out and its commander, Major Pietro Toselli, was killed. Ethiopian accounts link the assault with Fitawrari Gebeyehu Gora, who led Menelik's vanguard. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

The siege of the Italian fort at Enda Iyesus in Mekelle, from January 1 to 20, 1896, was much slower. Direct assaults failed with heavy losses. The Ethiopians finally forced the garrison under Major Giuseppe Galliano out by cutting it off from the springs outside the fort. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) That strategy is associated with Empress Taytu Betul (ጣይቱ ብጡል), who also led her own troops during the campaign. [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/battle-adwa-ethiopia-africa-colonization)

## The Battle on March 1, 1896

After Mekelle, Menelik went around the string of Italian forts toward the Eritrean border instead of attacking them, which raised the risk that Baratieri's supply lines would be cut. That threat, together with a sharply critical telegram from Prime Minister Francesco Crispi, led Baratieri to give up a planned withdrawal and march on the Ethiopian camp. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/battle-adwa-ethiopia-africa-colonization) Estimates of his combat strength range from about 14,500 to 17,800 men, with 56 guns. [Wikipedia: Battle of Adwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Adwa) [History of War](https://www.historyofwar.org/articles/battles_adwa.html)

The night march went badly for the Italians. Menelik had advance warning through the intelligence of the Eritrean double agent Basha Awalom, and the Italian columns under Generals Albertone and Dabormida became separated. Albertone's isolated brigade took the full force of the Ethiopian attack. By about 9:30 a.m. it had collapsed and Albertone had been captured, and the rest of the day became a pursuit. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

Casualty figures vary. Commonly cited estimates put Italian and colonial dead at about 6,000 to 7,000, with roughly 3,000 to 3,900 taken prisoner. Ethiopian dead are estimated at about 3,900 to 7,000. [Wikipedia: Battle of Adwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Adwa) [History of War](https://www.historyofwar.org/articles/battles_adwa.html) [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/battle-adwa-ethiopia-africa-colonization) The aftermath was brutal on both sides of the record. Captured Eritrean _askari_ soldiers who had fought for Italy were punished by amputation, which Bahru calls "a sore point" in the history of the victory. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865)

## Peace and Recognition

In Rome, Crispi's government fell, and Crispi resigned on March 9, 1896. [Wikipedia: Battle of Adwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Adwa) Under the Treaty of Addis Ababa, signed in October 1896 (sources give October 23 or 26), Italy annulled the Treaty of Wuchale and recognized Ethiopia as fully independent. In return, Italy kept Eritrea, roughly up to the Mareb river line, and Italy paid an indemnity to cover the upkeep of its soldiers held as prisoners. [Wikipedia: Treaty of Addis Ababa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Addis_Ababa) [Library of Congress Country Studies](https://countrystudies.us/ethiopia/15.htm) Other European powers followed by negotiating borders with Menelik as an equal sovereign. [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/battle-adwa-ethiopia-africa-colonization)

## Adwa and the Black World

Outside Ethiopia, many first explained the result away as "an Italian misfortune" rather than as Ethiopian military skill. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) In Africa and the diaspora, however, Adwa gave new force to the independent "Ethiopianist" churches of southern Africa and the Americas. The Haitian Benito Sylvain visited Menelik in 1897 and later represented Ethiopia at the first Pan-African Conference in 1900. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) Jonas argues that the victory opened a breach that, after the Second World War, fed the continent's struggle against colonial rule. [Harvard University Press](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674062795)

In Ethiopia the victory has been commemorated since the seventh year after the battle, and its centenary in 1996 was marked by events at home and abroad. [Bahru Zewde](https://journals.codesria.org/index.php/arb/article/view/4865) Adwa Victory Day (Yekatit 23) is a national public holiday. [Wikipedia: Battle of Adwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Adwa)

For the two people at the center of the campaign, see [Menelik II and Taytu Betul](/en/people/menelik-ii-and-taytu-betul). For the much older state that the northern highlands remember, see [the Aksumite Empire](/en/history/aksumite-empire).
